## Evaluation plan, before seeing new results

- Frozen M4 dev PILOT32 only for debugging and selecting a single candidate; compare all three checkpoints (v1/format/stop) with identical `transformers` policy, max_new_tokens=160, 6 steps, 5 tools, BM25 top3, no boosted titles, greedy Qwen3 non-thinking, same dataset SHA and model snapshot.
- Required metrics: correct answer EM; **strict verified answer+passage support**; completion/finish rate; error count; tool-budget exhaustion; duplicate reads/search; model calls/tokens/latency.
- If any candidate is at least competitive on strict verified success and materially reduces budget loops **without simply abstaining more**, freeze it and evaluate once on disjoint M4 eval-A64 (this eval-A has been used to assess the 8B guard, so is *not the final untouched independent test*).
- **Sealed final-B160 remains untouched** until future GRPO+RSI intervention and final evaluation protocol are fixed. Never retrospectively declare a pilot-only success a hidden-test generalization result.
- Training loss reduction is not scientific evidence of multi-hop QA or RSI. M5 is TRAIN-only SFT, **not GRPO**. Stop-policy improvement, if any, must be validated at equal budgets and real-model size.
